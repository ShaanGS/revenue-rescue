## Recording checklist

- Use synthetic Acme data and a test customer email address.
- Keep the browser zoom at 100% and turn off notifications.
- Start the screen capture before opening the local app; do not show code, tokens, or setup screens.
- If live connectors are used, show one returned record link for proof. Do not show private inboxes or unrelated workspace data.
- Keep the final file at or below the hackathon’s requested two-minute limit.
